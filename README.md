### 📈 ForecastIQ
**Demand Forecasting & Inventory Planning Dashboard**

Turns uploaded Excel/CSV sales history into forecasts, stock-out alerts, and suggested reorders, entirely in the browser with no data leaving the device.

- Custom damped Holt-Winters engine with auto-tuning and seasonality detection
- Confidence intervals (80/95/99%), what-if scenarios, WAPE & RMSE backtesting
- Reorder points, days of cover, and purchase-order suggestions
- Secure ingestion: Web Worker parsing, zip-bomb protection, locale-aware dates/numbers
